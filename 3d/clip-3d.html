    Bought, not printed: the small steel spring that sits inside the body behind the slider. The satin glove (seven sleeves joined by a band at the top) is sewn to fit and slid on after printing.

    What this model shows

    • A comb clip with one row of 7 tapered prongs and a softly rounded body that curves gently along its length to follow the head.
    • Colours to blend into hair: brown, black, red and blonde.
    • The prongs curve back to follow the head, so the clip grips and stays on.
    • Worn at the front of the head, just behind the hairline, with the slider facing forward. "On a model" shows it on a head of curly hair, with curls running back between the prongs.
    • The slider knob moves along its track and spreads or closes the prongs for different hair types.
    • Each prong is covered in satin from the body to the tip. The satin is one piece, like a glove: a band under the body joins all seven sleeves, so a single sleeve can't get lost, and it slides off in one go for daytime use and washing.
    • A light steel spring inside the body, behind the slider, so it grips without clamping. It can't be seen from outside; "Parts apart" shows it.

    Not decided yet

    • Real sizes: these are typical comb-clip sizes (about 8 cm wide, prongs about 4 cm long).
    • How the slider moves the prongs inside the body: shown here as a concept only. The printed comb has fixed prongs at medium spacing, and the printed body is solid, with no pocket for the spring.
    • How the satin glove attaches and how it stretches when the slider changes the spacing.
    • Spring tension: to be tuned in the founder's test nights.
